I was nesting so I planted a city

Wood, canvas, acrylic paint, scarlet runner beans, dirt, suitcase

Jasmine Johnson

When we see dried beans in a store, do we know where they came from? Do we know when their plants bore fruit, when their seeds were harvested? One day we'll plant our own seeds and pass them to our children, and their children, who will carry them over oceans and borders. They'll plant those seeds with whispered prayers for the future, just as they learned from their parents. A seed's roots withstands the passage of time, growing and producing again and again.
